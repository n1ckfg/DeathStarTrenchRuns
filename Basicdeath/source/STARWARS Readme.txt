1  --------About--------

This is a bird's eye view of you (x-wing) shooting down TIE fighters and Turbo Laser Turrets, and destroying the Death Star (or having it destroy Yavin 4).

3  -Program Description-

You are Luke Skywalker in an X-wing shooting at TIE fighters and Turbo Lasers.  Your X-wing can only shoot two shots at a time:  one from each wing at once.  Also, you have six homing proton torpedos for emergency situations.  It's not easy, because the Turbo Laser Turrets can lock on to you and fire shots aimed right toward you.  Your shield integrety is located on the right inside one of the trenches.  After a while, Han Solo comes in with his famous "Your all clear, Kid! Let's blow this thing and go home!" line.  Once that displays, you will see the thermal exaust port.  Do not try to rely on your homing torpedos because the only lock onto enemies.  Aim directly at the port.  If you are out of torpedos, you die, and you get to see the Death Star destroy Yavin 4.  If you are victorious, you get to see the Death Star blow up.  There is no scoring, but the game play is remarkably fast on a Silver Edition.  It is played on the graph screen.  Please make any modifications or use any parts of the code.  May the force be with you.
